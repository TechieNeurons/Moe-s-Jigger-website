Privacy Policy • Politique de Confidentialité

Moe's Jigger — Cocktail Recipe Book (Local Storage Only)

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the "Moe's Jigger" mobile application (hereafter referred to as "the Application") for Android devices. This document explains how data is handled within the application.

Summary: Your privacy is fully respected. The Application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything remains exclusively on your device.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The Application is designed to function entirely offline and locally:

  • No User Accounts: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the Application.
  • Local Storage: All cocktail recipes, modifications, favorites, or notes that you create are stored locally on your device's internal storage using standard Android storage mechanisms.
  • No Analytical Tracking: The Application does not use third-party analytics, tracking cookies, or diagnostic software to monitor your behavior or collect usage metrics.

3. Export and Import Functionality

The Application includes a feature that allows you to export your saved data into a JSON file, or import an existing JSON file to restore your recipes. This process is entirely manual and user-initiated. The exported file is saved locally to your device’s storage. No data is sent to external servers or cloud services during this process.

4. Third-Party Access and Sharing

Since no data is collected or transmitted by the Application, no data is shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to any third parties, advertising networks, or external service providers.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the Application does not collect any data from its users, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected directly by updating this page or within the application update notes.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official store listing contact channel or via GitHub.

1. Introduction
